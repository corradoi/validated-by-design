Validated-by-Design

A Shift-Left Approach to Computer System Validation for Cloud-Native GxP Platforms

Corrado Iorizzo · Steven Schmidt · Gupta Telluri

Read the PDF Release notes
Qualify for reuse. Inherit where justified. Prove continuously.

The proposition

Cloud-native platforms change continuously. Validation evidence and oversight must remain aligned with that operating state rather than being reconstructed only at the end of delivery.

The boundary

Automation and reusable platform controls can strengthen assurance, but they do not replace intended-use assessment, risk-based judgement, controlled change, exception management, or Quality accountability.
